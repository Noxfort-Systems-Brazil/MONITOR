# Noxfort Monitor — Audit Trail & Compliance

Noxfort Monitor records immutable operational, security, notification, and state-transition records across three specialized tables, exposed to `Operator+` users via `/audit` and `/api/audit/*`.

---

## 1. Security Audit Log (`SecurityAuditLog`)

Tracks every sensitive authentication, administrative, and infrastructure event in `security_audit_logs`.

* **Schema Fields (`internal/domain/audit.go`)**:
  * `id` (Primary Key)
  * `created_at` (UTC timestamp)
  * `username` (Authenticated actor or target username)
  * `action` (Canonical event identifier)
  * `details` (Contextual parameters and outcome)
  * `ip_address` (Origin IP resolved securely via `ExtractClientIP` and `TRUSTED_PROXIES`)

* **Key Audited Actions**:
  * Authentication & Lockout: `AUTH_LOGIN_SUCCESS`, `AUTH_LOGIN_FAILED`, `AUTH_LOGIN_LOCKED`
  * User Lifecycle & RBAC: `USER_CREATE`, `USER_PASSWORD_CHANGED`, `USER_ROLE_UPDATED`, `USER_UNLOCKED`, `USER_DELETE`
  * Infrastructure & Devices: `DEVICE_*`, `CONTACT_*`, `SETTINGS_UPDATED`, `TUNNEL_*`, `DATABASE_*`, `POSTGRES_USER_PROVISIONED`

---

## 2. Alert Dispatch Log (`AlertDispatchLog`)

Traces every notification attempt generated by `AlertService` across Telegram, Email (SMTP/TLS 465), and Webhooks in `alert_dispatch_logs`, including asynchronous retry backoff (`500ms`, `1500ms`) and graceful shutdown flush (`AlertService.Shutdown`).

* **Schema Fields**:
  * `id`
  * `telemetry_id` (Direct linkage to the triggering `telemetry.id` obtained via `SaveEventReturningID`)
  * `channel` (`telegram`, `email`, `webhook`)
  * `recipient`
  * `role`
  * `status` (`SENT`, `FAILED`, etc.)
  * `error_reason`
  * `dispatched_at`

---

## 3. Device State Transitions (`DeviceStateTransition`)

Captures historical SLA and connectivity changes computed by `StateManager` and `Engine` (`30s` tick, `5m` offline threshold) in `device_state_transitions`.

* **Schema Fields**:
  * `id`
  * `device_identifier`
  * `previous_state`
  * `new_state`
  * `duration_offline_sec`
  * `transition_at`

---

## 4. Access Control & Retention Lifecycle

* **Read Access**: Both `ADMIN` and read-only `OPERATOR` accounts can inspect audit records through `/audit`, `/api/audit/security`, `/api/audit/alerts`, and `/api/audit/transitions`.
* **Automated Pruning**: To prevent unbounded disk growth, `RetentionManager` automatically removes records older than `MONITOR_RETENTION_DAYS` (default **`90` days**) from `security_audit_logs`, `alert_dispatch_logs`, `device_state_transitions`, and `telemetry` every 24 hours.
